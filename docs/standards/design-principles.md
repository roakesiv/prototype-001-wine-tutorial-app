# Design Principles

## Core Principles for Wine Tutorial App

- **Tutorial-first, notes-second**: Prioritize guided learning experiences over personal journaling. Users should feel they're being taught, not just recording observations.

- **Structured learning**: Provide clear, step-by-step progression through wine tasting concepts. Avoid overwhelming beginners with too many options at once.

- **Reduce stress**: Use gentle, encouraging language. Frame tasting as a playful exploration rather than a test or evaluation.

- **Celebrate small success**: Acknowledge and reward incremental progress. Make users feel accomplished for simple observations and descriptions.

- **Explain why**: For each concept or step, briefly explain its purpose in the learning process. Help users understand the value of what they're doing.

- **Optional deeper help**: Provide additional information or advanced tips as optional layers, not required reading.

- **Direct description before poetic language**: Teach concrete sensory observations (sweet, tart, fruity) before introducing metaphorical descriptions (jammy, velvety).

- **Playfulness/silliness as learning reinforcement**: Use humor, fun analogies, and lighthearted elements to make learning memorable and enjoyable.

- **No homework feeling**: Avoid formal assessments, grades, or mandatory completion. Keep the experience casual and exploratory.
