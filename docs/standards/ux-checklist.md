# UX Checklist

## Screen Review Checklist

Use this checklist when reviewing each screen or interaction in the wine tutorial app:

- **Emotional feel**: Does the screen feel welcoming, low-pressure, and playful? Does it reduce intimidation?

- **Visual hierarchy**: Is the most important content/action clearly prominent? Are secondary elements appropriately de-emphasized?

- **Text density**: Is the text amount appropriate for beginners? Avoid overwhelming walls of text.

- **Action clarity**: Are buttons/links clearly labeled? Is the next step obvious without confusion?

- **Progressive disclosure/help placement**: Is complex information hidden until needed? Is help easily accessible but not intrusive?

- **Mobile usability**: Does the screen work well on small screens? Are touch targets appropriately sized?

- **Accessibility basics**: Can users navigate with keyboard? Is color contrast sufficient? Does it work with screen readers?

- **Success/celebration moment**: Does the screen acknowledge user progress or completion? Is there a moment of positive reinforcement?

- **Scope control**: Does the screen focus on one clear task? Avoid feature creep or too many options.
